Innehåll & Research

Demografia is a website dedicated to exploring and presenting demographic data from countries around the world, with a particular focus on Europe and the Nordic region. Our goal is to make complex population statistics accessible and easy to understand for everyone — whether you are a student, a researcher, or simply curious about the world.

The world is constantly changing. Populations grow, shrink, migrate, and age. These shifts follow patterns tied to economics, education, health, and policy. Understanding them is essential for understanding the future. On this site you will find rankings, comparisons, and stories behind the numbers — because every statistic represents a society, a culture, and millions of individual lives.

Demografia was built as a school project by three students with a shared interest in data, geography, and design. We have worked to present accurate information in a clean and modern format, easy to navigate on any device. We hope it sparks curiosity and contributes to a better-informed understanding of global society.
